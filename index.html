Getting started

Configure the scene, then let it run.

GMs configure scene items from the extension popover. The background runtime keeps evaluating even after the editor closes.

  1. 1
    Tag emitters

    Add one or more signals to items that advertise nearby facts.

  2. 2
    Create detector rules

    Choose a signal, range, response curve, and one or more effects.

  3. 3
    Choose targets and audiences

    Each effect independently decides what it acts on and who sees it.

  4. 4
    Keep playing

    The local runtime handles movement, scene changes, and cleanup automatically.
